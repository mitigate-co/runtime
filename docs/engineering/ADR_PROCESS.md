# Architecture Decision Records

Use ADRs for decisions that would otherwise be rediscovered or repeatedly debated.

Create `docs/decisions/NNNN-short-title.md`.

Template:

```markdown
# ADR-NNNN: Decision title

Status: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded
Date: YYYY-MM-DD

## Context
What problem/constraint forced a decision?

## Decision
What are we doing?

## Alternatives considered
What credible alternatives were considered?

## Consequences
What gets easier/harder? Security, cost, portability, operations?

## Verification
How do we know the decision works?
```

ADRs should be short enough to read and specific enough to matter.
